The recommendation

AI Solutions & Enablement Consultant

Personal brand descriptor: AI Builder with Data & Machine Learning Foundations

01 / Executive summary

Lead with the AI problem you solve—not the tools you use.

Kristin should not present herself primarily as a Data Analyst. Her strongest story is that she can understand messy business problems, structure information, work with data and machine-learning systems, and translate analysis into practical next steps.

Recommended one-line positioning: “I build practical AI-enabled workflows for better research, analysis, and decisions—grounded in data, designed for people.”
02 / Evidence already on the resume

Your data background is the proof layer.

The resume gives you more credibility than a generic AI-tool profile. These are concrete signals that you can work with ambiguity, quality, scale, modeling, and stakeholders.

04 / Search strategy

Search for the work, not just the title.

Titles vary significantly between companies. Combine role titles with the work you want to do: implementation, enablement, workflow design, adoption, research, and automation.

High-signal search phrases

AI solutionsAI enablementGenAI consultingAI transformationAI workflowAI adoptionAI strategy analystAI product operationshuman-in-the-loop AIbusiness automation

Signals to look for in job descriptions

  • Discovery of business processes and high-value AI use cases
  • Prototyping, testing, evaluation, and iteration
  • Workflow redesign and automation with human review
  • Adoption, training, documentation, and governance
  • Translating technical findings into measurable business outcomes
05 / Gaps to close

Make the AI claim demonstrable.

Codex and AI-assisted building are valuable differentiators, but they should appear alongside artifacts. The portfolio should show what you built, how it was evaluated, where human judgment stayed in the loop, and what improved.

Build next

  • AI Research Copilot: source-aware research, structured notes, evidence, and a decision brief.
  • Data-to-Decision Workflow: messy data to validated insight and executive-ready output.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Automation: repetitive knowledge work with review and QA checkpoints.

Add to the resume

  • Generative AI tools and APIs used in real projects
  • Prompt, workflow, or evaluation design
  • Automation or prototyping outcomes
  • Documentation, adoption, or stakeholder enablement
  • Responsible AI, privacy, and quality considerations
06 / 90-day plan

Turn positioning into evidence.

Research notes / sources

Why this direction is defensible

  1. Current AI transformation roles emphasize end-to-end work: discovery, solution design, implementation, adoption, and measurable business outcomes. See Fiserv’s AI Transformation Consultant role.
  2. AI enablement is not just tool access; it includes rollout structure, governance, and helping people turn capabilities into lasting operating practices. See OpenAI Academy’s AI Champion guidance.
  3. Practical workflow consulting starts with understanding where work is slow or repetitive, then designing automation with appropriate human review. See AI for Businesses’ workflow consultant overview.
